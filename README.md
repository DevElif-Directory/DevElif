# DevElif – Free Multilingual Website Directory

**Discover, explore, and list websites from around the world.**

[DevElif](https://develif.com) is a free multilingual website directory that helps users discover useful websites and gives website owners an opportunity to list their websites for free.

## 🌍 Available Languages

Explore DevElif in five languages:

- 🇬🇧 [English](https://develif.com)
- 🇪🇸 [Español](https://es.develif.com)
- 🇫🇷 [Français](https://fr.develif.com)
- 🇩🇪 [Deutsch](https://de.develif.com)
- 🇸🇦 [العربية](https://ar.develif.com)

## ✨ Key Features

- Free website submissions.
- Instant publishing.
- Dedicated public pages for listed websites.
- Direct dofollow backlinks.
- Website descriptions and categories.
- Five-language support.
- No account required to submit a website.

## 🚀 List Your Website for Free

Add your website to DevElif and get:

- A dedicated public listing page.
- Your website domain and description.
- Relevant website categorization.
- A direct dofollow backlink.

Visit [DevElif](https://develif.com) to discover more and submit your website.

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make discovering websites easier and help website owners increase their online visibility through a free, accessible, multilingual directory.
